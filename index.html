Sai's 4-step routine

Decode → Collect → Scaffold → Write

  1. Decode: say, “This question wants me to…” Then identify ACTION + TARGET.
  2. Collect: choose only evidence that directly proves the answer.
  3. Scaffold: build the thought in arrows before writing sentences.
  4. Write: answer, prove, explain. Stop when the job is done.
